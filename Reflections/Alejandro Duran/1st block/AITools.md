# AI Tools

## Claim

The AI tools are transforming the UI development process, it's true that increment the speed, but currently lack the ability to address complex usability atributes without human intervention.

## Data

Nowadays agencies as "Eleken" that is a UI/UX design agency, propuse an explainable AI that acts as a guide for user, what prove that AI is becoming in an important part on the user experience discuss.

## Warrant

Some things never change in humans, like perception and memory, so the interfaces generated by AI must still following to timeless heuristics like visibility of system status and some artefacts that we know that our users always will need.

## Backing

The studies about automation bias warning that an excesive automation on AI tools could reduce the positive results in the response of our users and lead to frustration.

## Qualifier

AI can produce high quality UI components quickly but can have difficulties with "Consistency and Standards" if the indications don't define explicitly the design system.

## Rebuttal

Some people believe that the UX role will disappear, but the need for a "heuristic evaluation method" that evolves with technology means that the function of UX role will be oriented to auditing and fine tuning AI outputs.

## Example

We explored using an AI tool to generate a Medical Dashboard.

Experience: It was easy to produce a visually pleasing layout, but the AI failed to include a "Back" button (User Control and Freedom).

We explore the use of a AI tool to generate a medical dashboard and we see that the experience was really easy to produce a pretty useful and atractive layout, but failed in include a back button, and result that the user interface was good on "Aesthetic Design" but failed at "Error Prevention", proving that the UX role remains critical for quality assessment.
