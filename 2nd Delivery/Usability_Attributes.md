# Usability attributes for the Medical App

---

## 1. Easy to Learn (Learnability)

This is the most important part for **Don José**. The app must be easy to use from the start because he is not used to hard steps in technology.

* **How it works**: The start screen (FR01) and labels on buttons (NFR-U-05) help the user find where to go without needing a book.
* **Goal**: The time it takes a new user to schedule their first appointment alone (Goal: less than 3 minutes).

---

## 2. Fixing Mistakes (Error Handling)

Don José is afraid of making a big mistake, and Elena gets worried when the system fails. The app must be kind and help the user.

* **How it works**: A warning before canceling (FR10) and using simple words (NFR-U-06) helps to stop accidents. It also explains how to fix internet problems (FR13).
* **Goal**: How many big mistakes the user can fix by themselves by reading the screen (Goal: more than 90% success).

---

## 3. Easy for Everyone to See and Use (Accesibility)

This makes sure workers and older teachers with tired eyes can use the app. It follows the standard rules of **WCAG 2.1, Level AA**.

* **How it works**: The text size can grow to 16px (NFR-U-01) and colors are easy to identify (NFR-U-02). It also has a way to log in for people who do not usually work with  their microsoft 365 email.
* **Goal**: A passing score on a test of these "easy to see" rules, defined by WCAG 2.1 AA.

---

## 4. Showing what the App is Doing (Feedback)

The goal is to stop "tech worry". The user must see a quick answer to know the app got their order.

* **How it works**: Small signs that show the app is "loading" (NFR-U-07) and messages that say "success" after every action. The app avoids still screens that make people wonder if it is broken.
* **Goal**: How fast the screen changes when you touch it. Note: To feel fast, it must happen in less than 5 seconds.

---

## 5. Less Brain Work

This stops people from feeling overwhelmed by only showing what is needed at each step.

* **How it works**: Showing information slowly (NFR-U-04) and only asking for 3 things per screen when schedule an appointment (FR07).
* **Goal**: How much "brain work" people feel when they interact with application, and scored a high happiness score (Goal: more than 80 points).

---

## Why this is good for the users

### Impact on "Don José" (Hard time with tech)

For him, this plan focuses on **Easy to Learn** and **Fixing Mistakes**. If he sees an error he does not understand, he will quit the app and go back to the office in person. This plan makes sure the app talks like him and leads him step by step (NFR-U-04).

### Impact on "Dr. Elena" (Tired eyes and speed)

For Elena, the plan focuses on **Less Brain Work** and **Easy Viewing**. She knows how to use tech, but she has no time or patience for messy screens. This plan makes sure she can read her papers without hurting her eyes (NFR-U-01) and can help her family with just a few clicks.
