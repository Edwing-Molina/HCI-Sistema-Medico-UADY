## **UX Metrics & Quality Attributes**

### **Operability / Intuitiveness**
* The degree to which the product has attributes that make it easy to operate and control.
Examples:
* **Key Indicators:** Minimal clicks, large buttons, and predictable actions.
* **Optimization:** Streamlined workflow for the rapid management of multiple dependents.
* **Target:** SUS Score > 80 points.

### **Accessibility**
* The degree to which the product can be used by people with the widest range of characteristics and capabilities.
Examples:
* **Key Indicators:** Useable even for those who do not master the digital ecosystem; functional under conditions of visual fatigue.
* **Compliance:** WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

### **Satisfaction**
* The degree to which user needs are satisfied, including physical, cognitive, and emotional responses.
Examples:
* **User Impact:** Don José feels capable and in control; Dr. Elena trusts the system to manage health data.
* **Target:** SUS Score > 80 points; positive "Trust and Confidence" scores in post-use surveys.

### **User Error Protection**
* The degree to which the system protects the user from making errors or allows for easy recovery.
Examples:
* **Key Indicators:** If a user enters incorrect data, the system guides them without blocking the process.
* **Safeguards:** Prevention of accidental appointment cancellations; mandatory confirmations before critical actions.
* **Target:** Successful error recovery rate > 90%.
